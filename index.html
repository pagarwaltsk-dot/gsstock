Four tabs, one for each job in the godown

Staff open the tab for what they're doing. Nothing else is on the screen to get wrong.

  • 📥
    Stock IN

    Goods arriving from the factory or a supplier. Give the list a name like "Raju thela" if you like.

  • 📤
    Stock OUT

    Goods leaving for a customer or the shop. Saved lists become a tick list the loading staff work through.

  • 🚚
    Transport

    Book goods by transport name and LR number. When they reach the godown, receive the full LR in one tap, or just the part that arrived.

  • 📋
    Activity

    Every entry and receipt, newest first, with who did it and when. Changing a saved list needs your PIN.
